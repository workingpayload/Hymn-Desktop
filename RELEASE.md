# Release notes

## v2.2.0 — 2026-11-25

A brand-new Home, and Hymn in Windows' media controls.

### New Home
- **A fresh look.** Today's date and a greeting up top, with Refresh and the List / Cards switch beside them. A featured carousel leads the page: play or shuffle songs, open albums and playlists. Move through it with the arrows, a trackpad or Shift + wheel.
- **Moods.** A row of mood chips (Pop, Hip-Hop, Chill, Workout, Focus, Party, Jazz and more) starts a queue for that mood, with the songs you're likely to love first. Hour-long compilations are left out.
- **Your daily mixes.** Up to four mixes, each grown from a different favourite of yours, shown as cover collages. They change from one day to the next (or when you press Refresh) and replace the old "Daily mix" shelf.
- **New from artists you follow.** Recent albums and singles from the artists you follow, newest first, so a new release doesn't pass you by.

### Windows media controls
- **Hymn shows up in Windows' media flyout** (by the volume and Quick Settings) with the song, artist, cover art and progress. Play, pause, next, previous and the seek bar there control Hymn.
- **Media keys go through Windows.** The keyboard's play/pause, next and previous keys now reach Hymn through Windows, so they control whichever player Windows is showing. Turning off **Settings → Global media keys** also takes Hymn out of the flyout.

### Also
- **Buy me a Diet Coke.** The support popup now shows up by itself every couple of days, at a random moment while Hymn is open. It never interrupts another popup, and the Diet Coke button in the dock still opens it any time.

### Notes
- Your settings, library, downloads and history carry over untouched.
- On macOS, media keys work as before.
